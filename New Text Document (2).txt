- Ways to Create a Positive Environment
	Promote Respect: Treat everyone with courtesy and professionalism.
	Encourage Open Communication: Share ideas, listen actively, and provide constructive feedback.
	Recognize Achievements: Celebrate individual and team successes.
	Support Teamwork: Foster cooperation and help colleagues when needed.
	Maintain a Positive Mindset: Focus on solutions rather than problems.
	Show Appreciation: A simple "thank you" can boost morale and strengthen relationships.
	Create Inclusivity: Ensure everyone feels welcome and valued regardless of their role or background
